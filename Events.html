Cultural Events & Festivals

Immerse yourself in the vibrant traditions of Sikkim. Explore a calendar full of colorful festivals and cultural events that showcase the state's rich heritage.

Saga Dawa Festival

A celebration of Buddha's birth, enlightenment, and nirvana. Witness colorful processions, prayers, and the lighting of butter lamps.

Ongoing: May 25th - June 23rd

Pang Lhabsol Festival

Witness warrior dances and prayers honoring Mount Kanchenjunga, the guardian deity of Sikkim.

August 28th, 2024

Flower Festival

A month-long extravaganza showcasing Sikkim's rich variety of flowers, including orchids and rhododendrons.

April 1st - 30th, 2025
